# Release notes

The public "What's new" text for each Play release, plus the internal release name used in the
console. The notes must stay under 500 characters per language. A new section is added at the
top for every release.

## 0.7.1

**Release name (console):** 0.7.1

**Play release notes:**

Translations and on-device rewrites now give only the answer: the model's thinking steps and stray
tags no longer reach the screen. The SIM button shows just the number, and the message box gets its
own row so long drafts no longer crowd the buttons. Fixes to backup and restore, and small layout
polish across the app. Everything still stays on your phone - no account, no ads, no internet
permission.

## 0.7.0

**Release name (console):** 0.7.0

**Play release notes:**

Files your messages automatically: codes, bank alerts, bills, orders, travel and promotions each
get their own inbox, with spend totals, search by words or by meaning, and on-device AI rewrites.
SMS and MMS with two SIMs: pictures from the same sender get their own thread, and a reply stays
in the thread it is sent from. Locked categories, encrypted backups, seven themes. Everything stays
on your phone - no account, no ads, no internet permission.
