## Full description (1,752 chars)

```
aismo files your text and picture messages for you, so you always know where a one-time code, bank alert, bill or delivery is - and it does all of this on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere: the app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no usage statistics.

What it does

- Inbox by category. 14 built-in categories (codes, bank alerts, bills, orders, travel, promotions and more) plus your own. Reorder them, hide them, mute them, and lock any of them behind your fingerprint or a PIN.
- One-time codes. Shown with a copy button, and removed automatically after you copy them if you like.
- Money. Bank alerts are summarised with their amount and merchant, and spending is totalled by month and by merchant, with budgets.
- Bills, orders and trips. Due dates and delivery status stay visible instead of buried.
- Scam warnings. Messages are scored and the likely ones listed for a weekly review; delete or block the sender in two taps.
- Search by meaning. "Power bill" finds the electricity notice, not just the exact word. The model is built in.
- Writing assistant. Rewrites your draft on the phone - fix everything, spelling, more polite, friendlier, or shorter - using a model file you download once yourself.
- Full messaging. SMS and MMS (pictures, video, group chats), long texts sent as one message, two SIMs, and an undo after you send.
- Private by design. No internet permission, no account, no ads, no statistics. Backups are encrypted with your passphrase and only go where you save them.
- Yours to look at. Light, dark or pure black, with seven colour themes.

aismo is a young app, and some behaviour (pictures and video in particular) can vary by phone and carrier. The full privacy policy is in the app, under About.
```
